# Gym Coach Agent

A chat-based coaching agent for gyms: each gym member talks to it directly, and it remembers their training across sessions.

## Language

### People & tenancy

**Agent**:
The software Members chat with.
It generates Routines, logs Sessions, and checks in proactively.
In member-facing chat it may present itself as "your coach", but in docs and code it is the Agent.
_Avoid_: Bot, coach (reserved for the human), assistant

**Coach**:
A human trainer at a Gym - a coach-flagged Member chatting through the same bot.
Edits the Gym's Rules doc and can hand-write Routines for specific Members.
_Avoid_: Trainer, staff

**Gym**:
The tenant every record belongs to.
A Member, their Routine, and their Sessions all live under exactly one Gym.
Moving to another Gym is a fresh start there.
_Avoid_: Club, location, tenant

**Invite code**:
The short random slug that links a new Member (or Coach) to their Gym on Telegram - carried in the Gym's deep link (`t.me/<bot>?start=<code>`) or typed as plain text.
One active, regenerable code per Gym; regenerating it invalidates the old one, including linking flows the old code started.
_Avoid_: Referral code, signup code, token

**Linking**:
Connecting a chat identity to a Member at a Gym - the deterministic conversation (Invite code in, name confirmed, gym switch confirmed) that runs before the Agent ever speaks for that person.
A gym switch creates a fresh Member at the new Gym and leaves the old Member row behind.
_Avoid_: Onboarding, signup, registration

**Member**:
A person who trains at a Gym and chats with the Agent.
Every Member belongs to exactly one Gym.
_Avoid_: Client, athlete, user

### Training

**Catalog**:
The product-wide list of Exercises.
Routines prescribe only Catalog names; a logged movement that matches nothing is added to the Catalog, never dropped.
_Avoid_: Exercise list, library

**Demo**:
The short soundless looping clip showing how an Exercise is done.
An Exercise may carry a default Demo; a Gym's own Demo for that Exercise wins over the default.
_Avoid_: Video, GIF, tutorial

**Exercise**:
A named movement (e.g. squat, bench press).
Workouts prescribe Exercises; Sessions record them; a Demo can show them.
_Avoid_: Movement, lift

**Gap**:
The days since a Member's newest Session - always derived by a query, never stored.
_Avoid_: Streak, absence, attendance record

**Intake**:
The conversation gathering exactly four things before a Member's first Routine: goal, experience level, training days and weekdays, and injuries or limitations.
_Avoid_: Questionnaire, survey, sign-up

**Preset**:
A named Routine a Coach saves once and applies to one Member, several, or the whole Gym; each Member on it holds their own copy, refreshed when the Coach edits the Preset.
Editing a Preset re-stamps every still-linked Member immediately; editing a Member's copy directly detaches that Member from the Preset for good. A Gym may mark one Preset as the default a brand-new Member starts on after Intake.
A Preset is retired, never deleted - Members keep the copies they hold.
_Avoid_: Template, program, shared routine

**Routine**:
The written training plan a Member follows - Workouts pinned to weekdays (e.g. Wednesday: legs).
Generated by the Agent under the Gym's Rules doc, hand-written by a Coach, or copied from a Preset (the latter two are coach-authored - the Agent never restructures those).
Exactly one Routine is active per Member; a replaced Routine is kept, deactivated.
The Agent may deviate from it on request.
_Avoid_: Program, plan, workout plan

**Rules doc**:
The plain-text coaching rules the Agent follows for a Gym: programme shape, set and rep schemes, progression numbers, and safety rules.
Exactly one doc governs a Gym - its own editable copy if it has one, else the shipped default.
_Avoid_: Config, policy, settings

**Session**:
One real gym visit by a Member - the record of what actually happened, including the lifts logged.
It opens when the Member says they're at the gym (or logs a Set), and closes when they say "done" - or auto-closes once abandoned long enough.
Known only from what the Member reports in chat; a Session with no Sets still counts as a visit, and it may not follow the scheduled Workout.
_Avoid_: Visit, workout log, training, attendance

**Set**:
One performed set of an Exercise within a Session: weight and reps.
Effort (RPE) and notes are stored only when the Member volunteers them.
_Avoid_: Rep scheme

**Weight suggestion**:
The next working weight the Agent offers for an Exercise, derived from logged Sets under the Rules doc's progression rules.
Ephemeral - spoken in chat, never written to the Routine.
_Avoid_: Target weight, prescribed weight

**Workout**:
The plan for one training day - a named list of exercises (e.g. "Legs: squats, lunges, calf raises").
Usually a day of a Routine, but can be improvised by the Agent on request.
_Avoid_: Day, workout day, training day

### Memory & outreach

**Check-in**:
The Agent's proactive outreach to a Member who has missed training.
Each Member's check-in state is on, off, snoozed until a date, or lapsed (wound down after ignored Nudges); any reply or new Session revives a lapsed Member.
_Avoid_: Reminder, follow-up

**Compaction**:
Folding a Member's oldest chat turns into one summary: durable facts land in Notes first, then the raw turns are deleted.
Compaction is the only chat-retention policy.
_Avoid_: Archiving, pruning

**Forget-me**:
A Member's hard delete of everything about them - training records, Notes, and chat history - immediate and residue-free.
Member-initiated only; the Coach is never told.
_Avoid_: Account deletion, deactivation

**Note**:
A durable fact a Member volunteered - an injury, preference, goal, or constraint - written by the Agent and readable by a Coach.
A safety flag is a Note of its own kind: the Agent logs it and pings the Gym's Coaches without asking, and a Coach's tick-off acknowledges it (who and when) without retiring it.
Never elicited; an outdated Note is retired (kept and dated, out of the Agent's recall), not deleted.
_Avoid_: Memory, annotation

**Nudge**:
One proactive Check-in message (e.g. "missed legs Monday - back on it today?").
Sent mornings only, capped per week, never on consecutive days.
_Avoid_: Ping, alert, notification

**Snapshot**:
The compact per-turn digest of a Member's facts - identity, Gap, last Session, today's Workout, active Notes - injected into the Agent's instructions.
When chat memory and the Snapshot disagree, the Snapshot wins.
_Avoid_: Profile, context
