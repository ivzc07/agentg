# Gym Coach Agent

A chat-based coaching agent for gyms: each gym member talks to it directly, and it remembers their training across sessions.

## Language

**Agent**:
The software Members chat with. It generates Routines, logs Sessions, and checks in proactively. In member-facing chat it may present itself as "your coach", but in docs and code it is the Agent.
_Avoid_: Bot, coach (reserved for the human), assistant

**Coach**:
A human trainer at a Gym. Adjusts the rules the Agent generates Routines by, and can hand-write Routines for specific Members.
_Avoid_: Trainer, staff

**Gym**:
The tenant every record belongs to. A Member, their Routine, and their Sessions all live under exactly one Gym. Moving to another Gym is a fresh start there.
_Avoid_: Club, location, tenant

**Invite code**:
The short random slug that links a new Member (or Coach) to their Gym on Telegram — carried in the Gym's deep link (`t.me/<bot>?start=<code>`) or typed as plain text. One active, regenerable code per Gym; regenerating it invalidates the old one.
_Avoid_: Referral code, signup code, token

**Member**:
A person who trains at a Gym and chats with the Agent. Every Member belongs to exactly one Gym.
_Avoid_: Client, athlete, user

**Routine**:
The written training plan a Member follows — workouts pinned to weekdays (e.g. Wednesday: legs). Generated by the agent or hand-written by a coach; the agent may deviate from it on request.
_Avoid_: Program, plan, workout plan

**Workout**:
The plan for one training day — a named list of exercises (e.g. "Legs: squats, lunges, calf raises"). Usually a day of a Routine, but can be improvised by the agent on request.
_Avoid_: Day, workout day, training day

**Session**:
One real gym visit by a Member — the record of what actually happened, including the lifts logged. Known only from what the Member reports in chat; a Session with no Sets still counts as a visit. A Session may or may not follow the scheduled Workout.
_Avoid_: Visit, workout log, training, attendance

**Exercise**:
A named movement (e.g. squat, bench press). Workouts prescribe Exercises; Sessions record them. An Exercise can carry a short demo (video/GIF) the Agent sends to show how it's done.
_Avoid_: Movement, lift

**Set**:
One performed set of an Exercise within a Session: weight and reps. Effort (RPE) and notes are stored only when the Member volunteers them.
_Avoid_: Rep scheme
